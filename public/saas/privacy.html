Institutional Privacy & Data Sovereignty Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Applies to all schools, academies, teachers, parents, and students

1. Core Guarantee: Absolute School Data Ownership

EduPulse operates strictly as a Data Processor under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. Each educational institution subscribing to EduPulse is the sole Data Fiduciary and retains 100% ownership and legal copyright over all uploaded student rosters, exam marks, financial ledgers, fee receipts, and staff records.

EduPulse never sells, commercializes, mines, shares with third-party advertising brokers, or monetizes student or parent data under any circumstances.

2. Information We Process

  • Institutional Identity: School name, logo, affiliation code, subdomain, and administrator credentials.
  • Student & Parent Information: Full names, admission numbers, dates of birth, blood groups, parent guardian names, emergency contact numbers, and postal addresses.
  • Academic Performance: Examination marks, report cards, class rankings, attendance marks, and grading metrics.
  • Financial & Fee Records: Fee categories, discount concessions, payment timestamps, receipt numbers, and transaction IDs. We do not store raw parent credit card numbers or UPI PINs.

3. Multi-Tenant Architecture & Data Isolation

EduPulse is engineered with strict multi-tenant data partitioning. Every database entry (students, fees, attendance records, notices) is strictly indexed by an immutable schoolId.

Queries and mutations are validated at the middleware layer. It is technically impossible for administrators, teachers, or parents of one school subdomain (e.g. school-a.schoolerp.in) to inspect, traverse, or query records belonging to another school subdomain (e.g. school-b.schoolerp.in).

4. Transactional WhatsApp & SMS Notifications

When schools configure automated WhatsApp or SMS triggers (such as student absence alerts, fee reminders, or exam results), mobile numbers and message bodies are passed securely to official WhatsApp Business Cloud APIs solely for delivery. No promotional messages are ever transmitted via these channels.

5. Backups, Data Export, and Right to Erasure

School administrators have the right and technical capability to download comprehensive data exports (JSON/CSV) of all student rosters, attendance logs, and fee ledgers directly from their School Admin portal at any time.

Upon contract termination or written request from an authorized school principal, all school-specific databases, backups, and media uploads are purged from our live server clusters within 30 days.

6. Security & Data Protection Officer Contact

We employ HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, hashed passwords using salted algorithms, and strict role-based access tokens for all session authentication.
