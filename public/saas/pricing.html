Frequently Asked Questions

How is our school's data kept separate from other schools?

Every school tenant operates with isolated record identification. A teacher or administrator logged into one school's subdomain cannot access, query, or view any records belonging to another school. Complete database exports can be created and downloaded directly from the Admin Portal anytime.

Do parents need to download an application from the Play Store?

No. We believe in zero-friction parent communication. Daily absence alerts, fee payment receipts, and exam schedules are delivered directly via WhatsApp messages and SMS to guardians' existing phone numbers, with links to the school's public website.

Can we use our own custom domain like portal.myschool.edu.in?

Yes. While every school is immediately provided an isolated subdomain (e.g. yourschool.schoolerp.in), Enterprise tier subscribers can map their custom institutional domain via a simple CNAME DNS record.
