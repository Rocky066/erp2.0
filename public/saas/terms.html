SaaS Master Subscription Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for all onboarded institutions and active subscriptions

1. Subscription & Service Provisioning

By creating a school account, subscribing to any EduPulse plan (Free Trial, Starter, Professional, or Enterprise), or accessing an assigned school subdomain, the institution agrees to be bound by these terms.

EduPulse grants the subscribing school a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the ERP software, modules (student admissions, attendance, fees, exams, portals, and notifications), and assigned subdomain in accordance with the subscribed tier.

2. Uptime SLA & Support Commitments

  • 99.9% Uptime Commitment: We maintain high-availability cloud infrastructure with automated database snapshots.
  • Planned Maintenance: System updates are scheduled outside standard school operational hours (typically between 1:00 AM – 4:00 AM IST) with prior advance notification.
  • Technical Support: Tiered support response times range from 12 hours (Starter) to 1-hour priority phone/WhatsApp response for Enterprise subscribers.

3. School Responsibilities & Acceptable Use

The subscribing institution is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of administrator and faculty access credentials. The institution agrees not to:

  • Attempt unauthorized penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, or probing of other school tenants.
  • Transmit malicious code, unlawful media, or defamatory content through the school portal or public school website.
  • Reverse-engineer or decompile any part of the SaaS codebase.

4. Billing, Renewal, and Price Protections

Subscriptions renew either monthly or annually as selected by the school. Annual subscriptions include guaranteed price lock protection for the duration of the multi-year cycle.

In the event of an expired subscription, schools receive a 14-day grace period during which portal access remains active to allow reconciliation without disrupting daily school routines.

5. Termination & Data Handover Protocol

Either party may terminate the agreement upon 30 days written notice. EduPulse guarantees that upon cancellation, the school administrator has full access to export all student ledgers, fee receipts, exam scorecards, and teacher profiles in industry-standard JSON and CSV formats.
