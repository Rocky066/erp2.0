Our Story & Mission

Pioneering High-Trust Cloud Technology for Indian Schools

We believe every educational institution deserves modern, high-speed, reliable digital infrastructure without heavy server investments or complicated maintenance.

Built for Practical School Realities

Schools in India face unique operational realities: academic sessions spanning different calendar cycles, complex fee concession policies, multi-language student records, and parents who prefer quick WhatsApp alerts over downloading complex mobile apps.

EduPulse was engineered from the ground up to solve these exact needs. From instant WhatsApp webhooks on attendance marking to session-isolated fee ledgers, every workflow is optimized for teachers, bursars, and principals.

🔒 Core Engineering Principles

  • 1. Zero Cross-Tenant Leakage: Strict database isolation ensures one school can never view or modify another school's records.
  • 2. Data Sovereignty: Schools own 100% of their student, financial, and faculty data. Full database backups are downloadable anytime.
  • 3. High Performance & Mobile First: Faculty portals run blisteringly fast on 4G/5G mobile browsers, enabling 60-second attendance marking.
