About Our School

Our Story & Mission

Established in 2023, Garumara Jatiya Vidyalaya was founded by a cooperative body of dedicated educationists who aimed to establish a high-quality Assamese Medium School in Garumara, Nagaon, Assam. We wanted to fill the gap of high-standard English-friendly Assamese education in the rural surroundings of Nagaon.

Over the years, the school has grown into a premier institution offering education from lower-kindergarten up to Class X, recognized for its standard curriculum, high teacher-to-student ratio, and consistent academic excellence.
