Student Admissions

Admission Guidelines

Admissions are open for Classes Lower Kindergarten (LKG) to Class X. Parents/Guardians who wish to apply for their wards must complete the online inquiry form or visit the school administration block on working days.

Age Eligibility Criteria

  • LKG / Nursery: 3+ Years on 1st April 2026
  • UKG: 4+ Years on 1st April 2026
  • Class I: 5+ Years on 1st April 2026
  • Classes II to X: Based on Transfer Certificate (TC)

Required Documents

  • Original Birth Certificate with 1 copy
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school
  • Aadhaar Card copy (Student & Parents)
  • 3 Passport Size Photos of Student
  • Caste Certificate copy (if applicable)

Fee Structure Overview

Class Group Admission Fee (Annual) Monthly Tuition Fee
LKG & UKG Rs. 3,500 Rs. 600 / Month
Class I - V Rs. 4,000 Rs. 750 / Month
Class VI - X Rs. 4,500 Rs. 900 / Month
